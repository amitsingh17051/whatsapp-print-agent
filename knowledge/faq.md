# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### 1. How do I print a document?
Send "Hi" or "Print" to start. Send your PDF document directly in this WhatsApp chat. Then specify how many copies and whether you want Black & White or Color. We will give you a price quote and payment link. Once paid, your print job will be sent to the printer immediately.

### 2. What file formats can I send?
Currently, only **PDF documents** (`.pdf`) are supported. Images (JPG, PNG) or Word documents (`.docx`) should be exported or saved as PDF before uploading.

### 3. How much does printing cost?
- **Black & White:** ₹2 per page
- **Color:** ₹10 per page
Standard paper used is A4 75 GSM.

### 4. Can I print double-sided (duplex)?
Yes! We support single-sided and double-sided printing. Double-sided printing saves paper.

### 5. How do I pay?
For this MVP preview, payment is handled via a secure test payment link/confirmation. Real payment gateway integration will be added in a future update.

### 6. Where do I pick up my printout?
Once printing is complete, you will receive a confirmation message on WhatsApp with your pickup code and desk number.

### 7. What should I do if my document fails to upload?
Make sure your file is a valid PDF under 25 MB and is not password protected. If you continue having trouble, simply upload the file again.
