# Product Specification

## Product

AI WhatsApp Print Agent

## Goal

Allow a customer to complete a printing order
through WhatsApp.

## MVP Flow

1. Customer sends Hi.
2. Bot displays available actions.
3. Customer chooses Print.
4. Customer uploads PDF.
5. System extracts page count.
6. Customer selects B&W or Color.
7. Customer selects number of copies.
8. System calculates price.
9. Customer performs test payment.
10. System creates print job.
11. Mock printer processes job.
12. Customer receives completion message.

## Supported Print Options

### Paper

- A4

### Color

- B&W
- Color

### Copies

1 or more

### Duplex

- Single-sided
- Double-sided

## MVP Payment

Use mock payment.

Do not integrate real payment yet.

## MVP Printer

Use MockPrinter.

Do not require physical printer.
